Egg drop.

The Egg Drop Project is a physics‑inspired game where players must prevent falling eggs from breaking as they drop from increasing heights. The game combines real‑world science with fun, fast‑paced gameplay, challenging players to react quickly, upgrade their equipment, and master different levels.

Players control a basket or catcher, moving it left and right to save the eggs before they hit the ground. Each successful catch earns points, while missed eggs reduce lives. As the game progresses, eggs fall faster, heights increase, and new obstacles appear, creating a dynamic difficulty curve.

The project includes a full game ecosystem

  • Lives system to track player survival
  • Game Over screen with score summary
  • Lobby and menu navigation
  • In‑game store for buying slow time, Extra live, Double score, Egg magnet, Egg shield and many more cards to come.
  • Missions and achievements to reward skill and progression
  • Online leaderboard to compete with other players

Inspired by real Egg Drop physics experiments, the game blends science, creativity, and strategy, making it engaging for both casual players and students learning about motion, impact, and design.
